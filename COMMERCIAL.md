AGPL requirements (notably source availability for network use) apply
as stated in the license text.

## Commercial License

A company or organization may separately obtain a commercial license
from the rights holders of the project if it needs a set of rights or
conditions different from the AGPL.

> Status note: dual licensing requires agreement of all copyright
> holders. Clearing contributor rights for a future commercial license
> is tracked as a separate step (see CONTRIBUTING.md).
